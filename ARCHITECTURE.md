# Architecture

## The product

The Owner writes a supermarket order in free Spanish ("necesito leche, dos cocas grandes y nada de huevos") and gets a Carrefour cart they only have to open and pay. The Assistant asks for the postal code, splits the order into Items, resolves each Item to Product Types of its own vocabulary with an LLM over evidence from the Catalog Snapshot, asks the Owner every open question in one message, and then picks one product per Item from Carrefour's live search and checkout simulation with the fixed rules of a Selection Policy. The LLM translates; code decides ([LLM](docs/LLM.md)). Terms are defined in [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md); the decisions are in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/README.md).

## Layers

The code is one package with the layers of ADR 0006 at the top and features inside the core.

- `src/core/<feature>/`: domain rules, use cases and port types of one feature. It imports only `core` and zod.
  - `src/core/catalog/`: text normalization, sizes in product names and brand matching.
  - `src/core/conversation-log/`: the events of a Conversation Log and what records them (`conversation-log.ts`): `loggedStore` decorates `OnlineStore`, `loggedSteps` records each step with its duration, `loggedTurn` records the Owner's message, the reply and how the turn ended.
  - `src/core/order-matching/`: the order matching flow being built beside the old one; for now the catalog product it reads from the Catalog Snapshot (`catalog-product.ts`): sold by weight with its Sale Step or packaged with its size, price, stock, department, Tipo and teasers; the `CatalogSearch` port (`catalog-search.ts`); and `matchItems` (`match-items.ts`), which searches each Item's Matches and keeps its Fits with `fit-products`, up to 10 calls at a time; none of it is called by the chat yet.
  - `src/core/order-conversation/`: the conversation of an order, `takeTurn` (`take-turn.ts`), and the step events it reports while it works (`steps.ts`).
  - `src/core/order-resolution/`: from the order text to resolved Items and questions; the `Llm` port; the eval set and its scoring.
  - `src/core/product-selection/`: from resolved Items to a cart; the `OnlineStore` port; postal codes; and, not yet called by the chat, the size and quantity rule that turns an Item's Fits into Candidates (`candidates.ts`) and the Selection Policy over simulated Candidates (`selection-policy.ts`).
  - `src/core/product-types/`: the schema and validation of the Product Type vocabulary and the Carrefour Tipo mapping.
- `src/adapters/<technology>/`: implementations of the ports. They import `core`, `node:*` and zod.
  - `src/adapters/carrefour/`: `OnlineStore` over Carrefour's public API ([Carrefour API](docs/references/carrefour-api.md)) and the conversion of Catalog Snapshot records.
  - `src/adapters/carrefour/`: also `downloadCatalog` (`catalog-download.ts`), which downloads the whole catalog for `pnpm carrefour:catalog`.
  - `src/adapters/catalog-search/`: `CatalogSearch` in memory over the Catalog Snapshot (`in-memory-catalog-search.ts`, ADR 0012): it leaves out products without stock and the departments Electro y tecnología, Automotor, Indumentaria, Juguetería y Librería and Aire Libre y Ocio, indexes each product's name, brand and Tipo, ranks by BM25 (`bm25.ts`) and by `pg_trgm`-style word similarity (`word-similarity.ts`) for the Item's `product` and, with a brand, also for `product` plus brand, and fuses every ranking by RRF (k = 60), ties in catalog order.
  - `src/adapters/claude-code/`: `Llm` over local Claude Code sessions, and the zod schemas that parse their answers. Its `onCall` reports each session with its task, model, the SHA-256 of its system prompt, its input, its raw output, cost and duration.
  - `src/adapters/files/`: every read and write of the data folders (`data-files.ts`), including the Conversation Log writer (`openConversationLog`) and the checked-out commit (`headCommit`). It is the only place that knows their paths and layout, and each loader of `vocabulary/` and `evals/` data runs the full validation of `core` (schema plus cross-references) and returns the data or the errors.
- `src/compose.ts`: the composition root. It wires adapters into the ports (`createLlm`, `createOnlineStore`, `createCatalogSearch` from the Catalog Snapshot, `createOrderResolver({ model? })`, `createConversationDependencies()` for `takeTurn`, `openConversationLog(sessionId, dependencies)` for the Conversation Log of one chat conversation), exposes the named loaders and savers of `src/adapters/files/` (`loadOrderData`, `loadProductTypes`, `saveEvalRun`, ...) and `downloadCatalog(outputDir?)`, and imports only `core` and `adapters`.
- `src/cli/`: one file per operator command, run by `package.json` through `tsx`. A command reads its arguments from `process`, loads and saves only through `compose`, never imports `node:fs` or `adapters`, and reports through `src/cli/report.ts` (print the errors and exit 1, or print the success line).
- `src/app/`: the Owner's chat (ADR 0008), a Next.js App Router app started with `pnpm dev`. `page.tsx` loads the order data through `compose` and shows the loader's errors instead of the chat when the data is invalid; `chat.tsx` is the client component; `api/turn/route.ts` is the `POST` route handler of one turn; `sessions.ts` is the server-only module that holds the conversations and opens each one's Conversation Log; `turn-events.ts` types the NDJSON events and `step-text.ts` turns step events into Spanish texts. It imports `core` and `compose`, never adapters.
- `scripts/`: repo tooling outside the architecture (`format.mjs`, `no-comments.ts`, `check-docs.ts`).

## Ports and the dependency rule

Three things are ports (ADR 0006, ADR 0012):

- `Llm` (`src/core/order-resolution/llm.ts`): split an order, resolve an Item, reword an Item, map an answer; `parseOrder`, which reads an order into the Items of `src/core/order-matching/item.ts`, and `fitProducts`, which keeps the Fits of one Item among its Matches, and `readReply`, which reads the Owner's reply to questions or a cart into entries per Item with the input types of `src/core/order-matching/read-reply.ts` (ADR 0012); none is called by the chat yet.
- `OnlineStore` (`src/core/product-selection/online-store.ts`): check delivery to a postal code, find candidates, simulate a cart, build the cart link.
- `CatalogSearch` (`src/core/order-matching/catalog-search.ts`): `findMatches({ product, brand }, limit)` returns the Matches of one Item, 30 by default, synchronously. It is a port because the search is planned to move to Postgres, not because two adapters exist.

Every method of `Llm` and `OnlineStore` that calls out takes an `AbortSignal`: the Claude Code adapter kills its `claude` process on abort and the Carrefour adapter passes the signal to `fetch`. `cartLink` only builds a URL and takes none.

Static data (Catalog Snapshot, vocabulary, mapping, eval set) has no port: it is loaded outside `core` and passed in as arguments. ESLint's `no-restricted-imports` in `eslint.config.js` enforces the rule in `pnpm lint`: `core` imports only `core` and zod; adapters import `core`, `node:*` and zod; `compose` imports `core` and adapters; `cli` and `app` never import adapters, and `cli` never imports `node:fs`. Imports between features inside `core` are free. The one exception is `resolve-order.ts`, which imports `bm25.ts` from `src/adapters/catalog-search/` until the old flow is deleted.

## An order end to end

An order is a conversation: `takeTurn(state, replyText, deps, onStep)` (`src/core/order-conversation/take-turn.ts`) takes the conversation's state and the Owner's reply and returns the next state and the reply to show. `deps` holds the order data, `Llm`, `OnlineStore` and the turn's `AbortSignal`, which reaches every port call; an aborted turn rejects and the caller keeps the state it had. `onStep` receives each step started and done (`src/core/order-conversation/steps.ts`): understanding the order or the answer, searching each Item, Carrefour candidates for each Item, simulating the cart. The chat in `src/app/` is the only caller that places orders.

The chat runs one turn per `POST /api/turn` (`src/app/api/turn/route.ts`) with the session id and the reply text. A new conversation gets its id from `crypto.randomUUID()` and the first event of its first turn carries it; the browser keeps only that id in React state. The handler claims the session in a `Map` held on `globalThis` (`src/app/sessions.ts`), answers 409 when the session is already working, calls `takeTurn` with `request.signal`, and streams NDJSON events (`src/app/turn-events.ts`): session id, step started, step done with its seconds, questions (the message text and each question's options, which the browser shows as chips plus "cualquiera"), cart, error. The browser reads them with `fetch` and `response.body.getReader()` (`src/app/chat.tsx`). A session is deleted when its cart is delivered, when its turn fails, and after one hour without a turn; an aborted turn, such as a closed tab, keeps the state from before the turn. Restarting `pnpm dev` or reloading the page starts a new order.

Every conversation is written, as it happens, to its Conversation Log. When `claimSession` creates a session it calls `openConversationLog` (`src/compose.ts`), which opens `.data/conversations/<local start time, YYYY-MM-DD-HHmmss>-<sessionId>.jsonl` through `src/adapters/files/data-files.ts`, writes `conversation-started` with the checked-out commit, and gives the session its own `Llm`, whose `onCall` writes each `llm-call`, and its `OnlineStore` wrapped in `loggedStore`, which writes each `store-call` with its input and output. The route handler runs each turn inside `loggedTurn` and passes `loggedSteps` beside the chat's step tracker, so the log gets `owner-message`, `step-started`, `step-done` with `durationMs`, `assistant-reply` (questions, cart or error) and `turn-ended` with outcome `completed`, `failed` or `aborted` and the error message. Each line is one event with an ISO `at` timestamp and a `type`. A write that fails is printed to the server console and the turn goes on; the log is never shown in the chat.

1. **Data.** Before the first turn, `createConversationDependencies` (`src/compose.ts`), called by `src/app/sessions.ts` when the chat page opens, runs `loadOrderData` (reading through `src/adapters/files/data-files.ts`), which loads the Catalog Snapshot's grocery products, the vocabulary and the Tipo mapping, and stops with every validation error before any order is read.
2. **Postal code.** A new conversation asks "¿A qué código postal es el envío?". `postalCodeFrom` (`src/core/product-selection/postal-code.ts`) reads the code from the Owner's reply; `OnlineStore.checkDelivery` asks Carrefour's regions endpoint (`src/adapters/carrefour/carrefour-store.ts`) whether it delivers there. A refusal asks again; once accepted, the next reply is the order text.
3. **Resolver.** `createOrderResolver` (`src/core/order-resolution/resolve-order.ts`) builds a BM25 index (`src/adapters/catalog-search/bm25.ts`) over the names of the products whose Tipo is mapped to a Product Type.
4. **Split.** `resolveOrder` calls `Llm.splitOrder`, which turns the text into Items (phrase, quantity, terms, vague words, brand) and flags a card or preference the Owner wrote, which is ignored for now (ADR 0011).
5. **Resolve each Item.** The BM25 evidence and the Item go to `Llm.resolveItem`, which answers `match`, `ambiguous` or `none` with Product Type ids; code drops unknown ids. `none` is retried once with `Llm.rewordItem`. Code then checks the Item's terms against product names and builds size options for vague words from the Catalog Snapshot (`src/core/catalog/catalog.ts`).
6. **Questions.** Every open doubt becomes one numbered message. `answerQuestions` maps the Owner's free-text answer onto the options with `Llm.mapAnswer` and asks again what still does not match.
7. **Pick.** `pickProducts` (`src/core/product-selection/pick-products.ts`) for each Item asks `OnlineStore.findCandidates` for available products of the mapped Tipos in the Owner's region, simulates the first candidates one by one with `OnlineStore.simulate`, and ranks them with the fixed Selection Policy (ADR 0011). When search finds nothing it falls back to Catalog Snapshot products.
8. **Cart.** The whole cart is simulated again; an unavailable product is replaced by the Item's next candidate. `OnlineStore.cartLink` builds the link, and the turn's reply is the Product Selection: each line, the notices, the total and the link.

## Data

| Folder | What it holds | Who writes it | Who validates it | Committed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `vocabulary/` | `product-types.json` (Product Type Groups and Product Types), `carrefour-tipos.json` (Carrefour Tipo to Product Type id, `null` for excluded) | By hand; no command writes them | Every loader in `src/adapters/files/`; `pnpm product-types` runs the same validation in `pnpm check` | Yes |
| `evals/` | `order-resolution.json`, the Owner's eval set: orders and what each must resolve to | By hand | The eval set loader, also against the vocabulary; `pnpm order:eval-set` runs it in `pnpm check` | Yes |
| `prompts/` | `order-resolution/<task>.md` and `order-matching/<task>.md`, the system prompt of each `Llm` task | By hand ([LLM](docs/LLM.md#changing-a-prompt)) | Measured by `pnpm order:eval`; not checked by `pnpm docs:check` | Yes |
| `.data/conversations/` | One Conversation Log per chat conversation, `<YYYY-MM-DD-HHmmss>-<sessionId>.jsonl`, one event per line | The chat (`pnpm dev`) through `openConversationLog` in `src/adapters/files/` | No validator; it is only read by people reviewing a mistake | No (`.gitignore`) |
| `.data/` | `carrefour/products.json` (the Catalog Snapshot; each product carries its first item's `measurementUnit`, `unitMultiplier` and promotion teasers, and `loadCatalogProducts` turns it into a catalog product), `order-resolution/eval-runs/` (one file per eval run) | `pnpm carrefour:catalog`, `pnpm order:eval` | No validator command; `src/adapters/files/` parses the Catalog Snapshot (a download older than its fields fails with a message to run `pnpm carrefour:catalog` again) and the eval runs with zod when they are read | No (`.gitignore`) |
